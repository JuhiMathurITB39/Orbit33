# User research

**Status: no interviews conducted.** Everything in PERSONAS.md is a hypothesis. This file is a plan and a blank template, not evidence.
## Interview guide (30 min, planners)
1. Walk me through your last weekly ordering cycle. 2. Which decisions take the most time? 3. When did a recommendation last surprise you, and what did you do? 4. How do you judge a supplier? 5. What would make you trust a system's order suggestion? What would make you ignore it? 6. What do you do when budget or storage is tight? 7. Which numbers do you report upward?
## Test tasks for usability sessions
Find the three items most at risk this week; decide an order for one and explain why; see the effect of a 7-day supplier delay; ask the assistant a question it should refuse.
