# Personas (hypotheses — not validated by interviews)

**Priya, Replenishment Planner (primary).** Owns weekly orders for ~2,000 SKUs across stores. Needs a short list of what to act on today, why, and how much. Pain: spreadsheet triage, surprise stockouts from late suppliers, no way to explain an order to her manager. Success: fewer firefights, faster weekly cycle. Trust test: "show me why and let me change assumptions."
**Marcus, Supply-Chain Manager.** Accountable for service level and inventory spend. Needs portfolio exposure, supplier risk and trade-offs (budget vs service). Pain: cannot see the cost of a service target. Uses the Control Tower, Supplier Intelligence and Scenario Simulator.
**Lena, Category Analyst.** Needs demand forecasts and seasonality to plan assortment. Uses Demand Intelligence; cares about forecast error and uncertainty, not orders.
**Dev, Data/ML Lead (internal reviewer).** Cares about leakage, backtests and monitoring. Uses MODEL_CARD, the model comparison table and the tests.

## Jobs to be done
"When stock is at risk, help me decide what to order and from whom, before the shortage happens." · "When budget is tight, show me which orders protect the most revenue." · "When a supplier degrades, show me what it does to my service."
