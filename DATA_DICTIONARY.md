# Data dictionary

**Provenance legend**: `M5-SCHEMA` = demand/price/calendar fields (real M5 if `ORBIT_M5_DIR` is set, otherwise produced by the seeded simulator `orbit/data/simulate_m5.py`; check `meta_kv.data_kind`: `SIMULATED_M5_SCHEMA` or `REAL_M5`). `SYNTHETIC` = always generated by `orbit/data/supply_sim.py`; M5 contains no inventory, supplier or purchase-order data.

## Raw inputs (M5 file schema)
| File | Grain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| calendar.csv | one row per day (1,941: 2011-01-29 → 2016-05-22) | `d`, `date`, `wm_yr_wk`, `weekday`, `wday`, `month`, `year`, `event_name_1/2`, `event_type_1/2`, `snap_CA/TX/WI` |
| sell_prices.csv | store × item × week | `sell_price`; absent before launch (kept as unknown, never back-filled) |
| sales_train_evaluation.csv | wide: item × store, columns `d_1…d_N` | units sold |
Simulated scope: 56 items (8 per department × 7 departments) × 3 stores (CA_1, TX_1, WI_1) = 168 series.

## Gold star schema (SQLite runtime; PostgreSQL DDL in `sql/postgres/001_schema.sql`)
**dim_date** (M5-SCHEMA) `date_key` yyyymmdd PK, `date`, `d_idx` 0-based, `wm_yr_wk`, `weekday`, `wday` (M5: 1=Saturday), `month`, `quarter`, `year`, `is_weekend`, events, `snap_ca/tx/wi`.
**dim_product** (item id, department, category: M5-SCHEMA; the rest SYNTHETIC) `item_id`, `dept_id`, `cat_id`, `supplier_id`, `unit_cost` (SYNTHETIC, a fraction of price), `ref_price` (median observed price), `pack_size`, `moq_units`, `unit_volume`.
**dim_store** `store_id`, `state_id` (M5-SCHEMA); `warehouse_key`, `storage_capacity_volume` (SYNTHETIC).
**dim_warehouse** (SYNTHETIC) 2 distribution centres: DC_WEST (serves CA_1), DC_CENTRAL (TX_1, WI_1).
**dim_supplier** (SYNTHETIC, SCD type 2) 8 suppliers S01–S08; `version`, `valid_from`, `valid_to` (9999-12-31 = open), `is_current`, `mean_lead_days`, `lead_sd_days`, `on_time_prob`, `weekly_capacity_units`. S03 and S06 change terms on 2014-01-01 (lead time up, on-time probability down).
**fact_sales** (M5-SCHEMA) PK (`date_key`, `product_key`, `store_key`); `units_sold` ≥ 0 integer, `sell_price`, `sales_amount`, `is_outlier` (flag only; values are kept).
**fact_inventory** (SYNTHETIC) same grain; `on_hand_end`, `on_order_end`, `units_received`, `units_demanded` (= units sold: see assumption below), `units_fulfilled`, `lost_sales_units`, `stockout_flag`. Invariant: demanded = fulfilled + lost; on-hand ≥ 0.
**fact_purchase_orders** (SYNTHETIC) `po_id`, product/store/supplier/warehouse keys, order/promised/received date keys, `qty_ordered`, `qty_received`, `unit_cost`, `status` (open/received/partial), `promised_lead_days`, `actual_lead_days`, `delay_days`, `is_late` (late = delay ≥ 2 days).
**fact_replenishment** (SYNTHETIC) the baseline policy's decisions: `inventory_position`, `qty_ordered`, link to `po_id`.

## Operational tables
`etl_checkpoint` (stage, batch, status, input hash, rows, attempts), `dq_results` (layer, table, check, passed, n_failed, detail), `pipeline_runs`, `data_lineage`, `meta_kv` (e.g. `data_kind`, `last_train_at`).

## Serving tables (model outputs, written by `orbit.ml.train`)
`serving_forecast` (point, 80% lo/hi, model, run), `serving_backtest` (origin, actual, prediction, role validation/test), `serving_stockout_risk` (probability, flag, threshold, position fields, forecast7, revenue_at_risk), `serving_supplier_risk` (recent late rate, lead mean/sd, predicted late probability, risk level), `serving_metrics` (JSON blobs: forecast_comparison, seasonality, stockout_model, supplier_model, simulation).

## Key assumptions embedded in the data
1. Units sold are treated as **uncensored demand** (real M5 sales are censored by stockouts that M5 does not record).
2. The simulated baseline policy is "when days of cover < 12, order up to 24 days"; the inventory history is the outcome of that policy.
3. Cost parameters (holding 25%/yr, $12 per order, lost margin plus 25% goodwill) are assumptions, not measurements.
